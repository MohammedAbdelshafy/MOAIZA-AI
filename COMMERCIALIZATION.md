# MOAIZA AI Commercialization

## Product
AI-assisted construction tender analysis for Egypt/GCC construction businesses.

## Core buyer outcome
Reduce manual tender review effort by extracting BOQ information, highlighting risks, estimating costs, and packaging bid analysis.

## Pilot
One tender package supplied by an authorized customer.

## Deliverables
- source-file inventory
- extracted BOQ with confidence
- risk flags
- cost model
- bid analysis
- generated report

## Commercial gates
- Source and parser confidence must be visible.
- Human estimator review remains part of the workflow.
- AI-generated estimates are advisory unless independently verified.
- Customer documents remain protected and are never used as public fixtures.

## Expansion
Team seats, recurring tender volume, reports/API, supplier price integrations, and historical-bid intelligence.
